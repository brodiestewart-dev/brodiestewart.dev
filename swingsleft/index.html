Swings Left

How many swings until you finish up?

Swings Left works it out from your roster and keeps the count on your home screen and lock screen. On site it shows "36 + this one", and it ticks over by itself every day.

What it does

  • Any roster: 8/6, 9/5, 14/7, 14/14, 5/2 or your own.
  • Swings, shifts and days left to any date: a contract end, a holiday or finishing up for good.
  • Your call on travel days: count your fly-in and fly-out days as shifts, or not.
  • Leave: take any swing out of the count.
  • Widgets for the home screen, lock screen and StandBy, including a full-page widget.
  • Premium (A$0.99, one-off): no ads, more than one countdown, your own photo backgrounds and colours.
