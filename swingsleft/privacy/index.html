Swings Left privacy policy

Effective 27 November 2026

Swings Left is made by Brodie Stewart, a sole trader in Queensland, Australia ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

The short version

  • Your countdowns, roster, leave and background photos stay on your iPhone. I don't have a server, and I never see them.
  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to show and measure ads.
  • Premium removes the ads, and with them Google's ad data collection.

Information stored on your device

Your countdowns (titles, dates, roster, leave and display choices) and any photo you choose as a background are saved on your iPhone, in storage shared only with the Swings Left widgets. Photos are picked with Apple's photo picker, so the app only gets the photos you choose, not your library. A copy is shrunk to widget size and kept in the app. None of this is sent to me or anyone else. If you use iCloud or iPhone backups, it may be included in your own backup, which Apple holds under its privacy policy.

Advertising (free version)

The free version uses Google AdMob to show a banner ad. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, app version and IP address (used for approximate location).
  • Ad interactions, such as ads shown and tapped.
  • Your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking when the app asks.

If you choose Ask App Not to Track, Google shows ads that aren't based on tracking. You can change this any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Google's use of this information is covered by Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Premium is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. I receive confirmation that the purchase was made, but not your payment details.

Sharing

I don't sell your information and I don't share it with anyone, apart from the ad data Google collects directly in the free version, as described above.

Children

Swings Left is made for adults who work rosters and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete a countdown or photo in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.
  • Turn off tracking for the app in iPhone Settings.
  • Buy Premium to remove ads and the ad data collection that comes with them.

If you have a question or a complaint about privacy, email support@brodiestewart.dev. If you're not satisfied with my response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
