Common questions

How are swings and shifts counted?

From the first day of your next (or current) swing, Swings Left repeats your roster until your end date. Every day on site counts as a shift, unless you switch off your travel-in or travel-out day in the countdown's settings. Days on or after the end date aren't counted.

What does "+ this one" mean?

While you're on site, "36 + this one" means 36 more swings after the one you're on now. You can change it to show the total (37) instead.

I'm taking leave. How do I take a swing out?

Open the countdown and switch on leave for that swing. It comes off your swings and shifts.

My widget is grey or hasn't updated.

Open Swings Left once. That refreshes every widget. If it's still grey, email me with a screenshot and your iPhone model.

How do I choose which countdown a widget shows?

Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, and pick the countdown.

I bought Premium on another phone.

Go to Settings › Premium › Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

I have a code for Premium.

Go to Settings › Premium › Redeem a code.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your iPhone only. Deleting a countdown removes it, and deleting the app removes everything.
